Our Story & Vision

Founded on faith, built on community, and dedicated to the spiritual growth of Broward County.

Heritage & Heart

A Heart for Broward, A Soul for Service

Bethel Casa De Dios began as a small gathering of families in a living room, united by a shared hunger for authentic worship and a deep-seated love for our neighbors in Broward County.

Over the years, we have grown into a multi-generational community committed to a clear mission: to evangelize, disciple, affirm, and send.

We evangelize by sharing the message of hope with our community.
We disciple by helping people grow in their faith through teaching and connection. We affirm by creating a welcoming environment where every person is valued and loved. And we send by equipping individuals to live out their purpose beyond the walls of the church. 


We believe that every person who walks through our doors is not just a guest, but a valued member of a family ready to welcome them home.

"Where every soul finds a place to rest."
